# 2. Visual Structure

## Titles and Headings
* Meaningful headings; like including time period.
* Subheadings
* Title Hierarchy
* Choice of axis, values, titles and chart type.

## Visual Hierarchy
* Mention the visualise hierarchy; title, visualisation, axis names, etc.
* Don't make every element equally prominent
* Mention colours/size and how they affect the audience to show importance.

## Grouping Information
* Place related stats next to charts.
* Have filters near what they affect.

## Chart Order
* Yearly (if multiple years), monthly and quarterly pivot charts when given time/date.
* Have to write about format of numbers in a chart.
