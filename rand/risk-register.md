# Risk Register for a Dataset

Kaggle was used to find the dataset and then a dataset based on data from the website Amazon was chosen. 

[Kaggle Link](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/talalhakem/amazon?resource=download)

## Risk Register

|Risk ID|Risk|Impact Desc|
|-|----|----|
|A|Untrusted author|An untrusted author could mean malicious files uploaded or even false information.|
|B|Fake dataset|This will mean there is no good analysis.|
|C|Unclean dataset|Analysis will be inaccurate or time will be taken to clean the dataset.|
|D|Unnecessary data included|Waste of storage and time used to remove\clean up the data.|
|E|Data lost in loading|This may cause issues in the reliability and usability of the data.|
|F|Different standards for regions|This could lead to errors in the data due to different standards being used, like how Americans sue a different date format.|
|G|Special characters not supported|Data lost can lead to whole sections of data being scrapped or lose meaning.|
